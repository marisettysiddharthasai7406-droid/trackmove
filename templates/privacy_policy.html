Privacy Policy

Your privacy and information security are important to us.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TrackMove. TrackMove is a public transport tracking platform designed to help users view bus information and track public transport services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use TrackMove and how that information is used.

2. Information We Collect

When you create and use a TrackMove account, the platform may collect basic account information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your account information

3. How We Use Information

The information collected by TrackMove may be used to:

  • Create and manage your TrackMove account.
  • Provide access to dashboard and tracking features.
  • Improve the functionality and user experience of the platform.
  • Respond to questions or feedback submitted through the Contact page.

4. Bus Tracking Information

TrackMove displays information related to public transport buses, including bus numbers, routes, locations, and availability status.

This information is intended to help users understand current public transport activity through the TrackMove platform.

5. Account Security

Users should keep their account credentials private and should not share their passwords with other people. TrackMove users are responsible for maintaining the security of their account information.

6. Information Sharing

TrackMove does not intentionally display private account information such as a user's password to other users. Account information is used to provide the services available through the platform.

7. Cookies and Sessions

TrackMove may use browser sessions to maintain login status and provide access to features that require authentication. These sessions help the platform recognize a logged-in user while they use the website.

8. Third-Party Services

TrackMove may use external services or technologies in the future to support features such as maps, hosting, or other platform functionality. The privacy practices of those services may be governed by their own policies.

9. Children's Privacy

TrackMove is designed as a general public transport tracking platform. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information through forms or messages.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TrackMove adds new features or changes how information is handled. Updated information will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, suggestions, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the TrackMove Contact page.
